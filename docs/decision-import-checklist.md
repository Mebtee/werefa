## What "Imported" Means

A decision is fully imported when:

- Its canonical wording is recorded (no `NEEDS SOURCE DECISION TEXT`).
- Its domain is assigned.
- Its mapping to the decision register is complete (`DEC-*` ↔ `KF-*` where applicable).
- It has produced one or more requirements (or is explicitly recorded as producing none, with rationale).
- All affected documents are identified.
- Any conflict or duplicate is recorded.
