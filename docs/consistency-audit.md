## 2. Method

The audit proceeded in fixed steps:

1. **Full read** of all ten documents (master spec read end-to-end; register read end-to-end including all 15 `KF-*` groups).
2. **Fact-to-requirement verification** — every `KF-*` reference in every requirement exists in the register (verified programmatically: 242 unique `KF-*` references in the spec/matrix, all present in the register).
3. **Requirement numbering check** — REQ-001 … REQ-226 contiguous, no gaps, no duplicates (verified programmatically: PASS).
4. **Explicit contradiction search** — pairwise scan of fact statements and requirement statements for mutually exclusive behaviors (e.g., sort keys, channels, durations, state sets).
5. **Semantic-conflict review** — near-miss pairs whose wording differs but may or may not conflict (recorded with outcome).
6. **Duplicate detection** — same fact captured under multiple IDs; same requirement intent captured twice.
7. **Gap analysis** — behaviors referenced by facts but not (fully) represented in requirements; clarifications required by this audit.
8. **State-machine analysis** — booking states, payment states, and slot-lock; every transition checked for trigger/actor/conditions/notifications; unsupported transitions marked `STATE-MACHINE DECISION REQUIRED`.
9. **Cross-domain checks** — tenant isolation, date/time rules, subscription lifecycle, pause/resume combinations, schedule exceptions, notification matrix, report sorting.
10. **Quality review** — typos, weak MUST/MAY language, vague statements, non-testable criteria, missing actor/tenant/conditions/outcomes, assumptions presented as facts.
11. **Recording** — every finding below is traceable to a document/section; every change made as a result is listed in Section 9.

**Rules applied throughout:** do not invent behavior; do not force-closure of open questions; do not invent durations; do not fabricate `DEC-*` mappings; preserve history when resolving conflicts.

### 8.5 Schedule Exception Consistency
